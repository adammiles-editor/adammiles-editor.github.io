02 / The psychology behind the edit

Attention with a purpose

I don’t just cut clips.
I think about why people watch.

Every edit is shaped around the way people naturally pay attention. The goal isn’t to make a video feel busy. It’s to keep the viewer curious, make the message easy to follow, and let the right moments land.

01

Create curiosity

I use strong openings, unanswered questions, and well-timed reveals to give viewers a reason to keep going.

02

Reset attention

Changes in visuals, sound, and pacing arrive when attention may start to drift, not simply because another effect looks cool.

03

Make it easy to follow

Clean structure and visual emphasis reduce confusion, helping viewers understand the story without working for it.

04

Let emotion land

Not every moment needs a fast cut. Sometimes a pause, a reaction, or a quieter beat is what makes the message memorable.

01
